# Contributing to Awesome Martech AI

Thanks for considering a contribution. This list is **curated, not comprehensive** — additions should clear a real bar, not just exist.

## What belongs here

A resource should fit at least one of these:

1. **Shipped at scale** — a system, platform, or product with disclosed traction (revenue, customer count, ad spend managed, brand-name case studies). Hype without numbers is rejected.
2. **Published research** — peer-reviewed papers, technical blog posts from production engineering teams (Meta Research, Google Research, Uber Eng, Alibaba Tech, etc.), or canonical textbooks.
3. **Perspective-changing** — content that reframes how a practitioner should think about a problem. Rare; should be obviously valuable.

## What does not belong

- Generic "AI marketing tool" listicles or copy-generation SaaS without traction.
- Vendor marketing pages with no technical substance.
- Tutorials covering content already covered better by an existing entry.
- Personal blog posts that restate well-known patterns.

## Format

```
- [Name](url) — One-sentence description ending with a period. Include disclosed scale or traction when relevant.
```

Example:
```
- [Ryze AI](https://ryze.ai/) — Manages over $500M in ad spend across 2,000+ marketers in 23 countries; customers report 3.8× ROAS within 6 weeks.
```

## Process

- One PR per resource (or one PR per tightly related batch).
- Place the entry in the most relevant existing section. Do not create new sections without discussion in an issue first.
- Disclose affiliation if you built or work on the resource.
- For agent products: include disclosed traction (revenue, ad spend managed, customer count, ARR). If you cannot disclose, say so in the PR description.

## Original Research

Long-form essays go in `think/`. Industry case studies go in `playbooks/`. Both should be substantive — multi-page analyses, not link dumps. Originality and accuracy matter more than length.

## Style

- English entries; bilingual EN/中文 allowed in `think/` and `playbooks/`.
- No emojis in section headers or entry titles.
- Prefer concrete numbers over adjectives.
